Privacy Policy

Game: Merge Defense · Developer: Daniil (independent developer)
Contact: danko9172@gmail.com
Last updated: 18 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you play the browser game Merge Defense (the “Game”), why it is processed, and what rights you have. The Game is developed by Daniil, an independent game developer (“we”, “us”, the “Developer”), who is the data controller for the limited processing described below.

Short version: the Game has no servers of its own and no accounts of its own. We do not collect your name, email, address or payment details. Your progress is stored in your browser and in the cloud save service of the platform you play on. Advertising and payments are handled by the platform (CrazyGames) and its payment provider (Xsolla) under their own privacy policies.

1. Information the Game processes

1.1 Game progress and settings

The Game stores your progress locally in your browser (localStorage): completed levels and worlds, unlocked units and spells, in-game currency and item balances, the “Remove Ads” flag, tutorial state, language and sound settings.

If you are signed in on the hosting platform, the same data is also saved to that platform’s cloud save service (on CrazyGames — the SDK “Data” module), so your progress follows you between devices. This data is game data only; it contains no personal identifiers written by us.

1.2 Data received from the platform

When the Game runs on a platform, the platform’s SDK may provide us with:

DataWhy we use it
Whether you are signed in, and a platform user identifier / usernameTo attach cloud saves and delivered purchases to the correct account
System locale (e.g. “en”, “ru”)To display the Game in a suitable language
Platform sound/mute settingTo respect your audio preference

We use this data only inside your play session. We do not build player profiles, we do not send this data to any server of our own (we operate none), and we do not sell or share it.

1.3 Purchase information

If you make an in-game purchase, the payment is processed by the platform and its payment provider — on CrazyGames this is Xsolla, acting as the authorised merchant. Your card number, bank details and billing information are entered in the provider’s payment interface and are never received or stored by us. We only receive confirmation that a given order was paid, so that the corresponding virtual items can be delivered to your account. If you contact us about a purchase, we will process the information you choose to send us (such as an order number and your email address) solely to resolve that request.

1.4 Advertising

The Game shows interstitial and optional rewarded ads served by the hosting platform and its advertising partners. Ad selection, frequency capping and measurement are performed by those partners, who may use cookies, device identifiers and similar technologies under their own privacy policies. We do not have access to that data and we do not run our own ad or tracking scripts inside the Game.

1.5 Analytics

The Game does not include any analytics SDK of the Developer. The platform may collect its own gameplay and traffic statistics (for example, gameplay start/stop events reported through its SDK) in aggregated form, under its own privacy policy.

2. Legal bases for processing (GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract — storing and syncing your game progress, and delivering purchased virtual items (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the Game working correctly, preventing fraud and abuse of purchases (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Consent — for personalised advertising, where consent is required; this consent is collected by the hosting platform, which is also responsible for withdrawing it.

3. Who else is involved

The Game depends on the following third parties, each of which acts under its own privacy policy:

  • CrazyGames — hosting platform: accounts, cloud saves, advertising, platform analytics. crazygames.com/privacy
  • Xsolla — payment processing and merchant of record for in-game purchases. xsolla.com/privacypolicy
  • Where the Game is published on other portals (Yandex Games, Playgama and similar), the corresponding platform’s privacy policy applies to that version of the Game.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Storage and retention

  • Local progress stays in your browser until you clear your browser data or reset progress in the Game.
  • Cloud saves are stored by the platform for as long as your platform account exists and according to that platform’s retention rules.
  • Purchase and transaction records are retained by the payment provider for the period required by tax and accounting law.
  • Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request and to keep a record of purchase-related correspondence.

5. International transfers

Our third-party partners operate globally and may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Those transfers are carried out by the respective partner under the safeguards described in its own privacy policy (such as Standard Contractual Clauses).

6. Children’s privacy

The Game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact danko9172@gmail.com and we will delete anything we hold. Where a platform designates content as child-directed, advertising is served in a non-personalised, child-appropriate mode by that platform.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of your personal data, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

Because we hold almost no personal data ourselves, most requests are fastest to handle directly:

  • Delete your game progress — clear your browser data for the game page, and/or delete your data through the platform account settings.
  • Account, cloud save or advertising data — contact the platform (CrazyGames) directly.
  • Payment data, invoices, refunds — contact the payment provider (Xsolla) at help.xsolla.com.
  • Anything else, or if you are not sure who to ask — write to danko9172@gmail.com and we will help, and forward the request to the right partner if needed. We respond within 30 days.

8. Security

The Game is served over HTTPS by the hosting platform. We do not operate servers or databases containing player data, which significantly limits the risk of a data breach on our side. No method of transmission or storage on the internet is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data processed by third-party platforms.

9. Cookies and local storage

The Game itself uses browser local storage strictly to save your progress and settings — this is essential for the Game to function and is not used for tracking or advertising. Cookies and similar technologies used for advertising, sign-in and platform analytics are set by the hosting platform and its partners, and can be managed through their consent tools and through your browser settings.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the Game, its partners, or the applicable law change. The current version is always available at this address, with the “Last updated” date at the top. Material changes will be reflected by updating that date.
